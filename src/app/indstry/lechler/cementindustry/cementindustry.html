❄️

Gas Conditioning Towers (GCT)

Complete 100% water droplet evaporation without wet bottoms. Drops flue gas temperature from ~450°C to 180°C to protect baghouse filter fabrics and stabilize electrical resistivity for electrostatic precipitators.

Lechler Spillback & VarioJet® Lances
🧊

Grate Clinker Cooler Spray

Dual downward water spray lances inside the cooler exhaust hood reduce gas temperature from 350°C to 180°C, protecting the downstream cooler baghouse and reducing cooling fan power.

High-Pressure VarioJet® Lances
🔻

Downcomer Duct Quenching

Rapid in-duct water injection quenches high-velocity flue gas between cyclones and mill, shrinking gas volume without needing a separate tower.

In-Duct Quench Arrays
🔥

Calciner SNCR DeNOx Injection

Aqueous ammonia or urea injected into the calciner thermal window (850°C–1050°C), achieving up to 88% NOx reduction with under 5 ppm ammonia slip.

Twin-Fluid VarioJet® Atomizers
