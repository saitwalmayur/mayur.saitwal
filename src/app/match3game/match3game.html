How to Play 3D Match-3 Quest

🎮 Basic Controls & Limited Lives

Click or drag adjacent gems to swap. You start with 5 Lives ❤️. Losing a level consumes 1 life, which regenerates automatically every 15 minutes!

✨ Special Combinations

  • Match 4: Creates a Rocket Gem that clears an entire row or column!
  • Match 5: Creates a Rainbow Prism! Swap it with any gem to clear all gems of that color!
  • T or L Shape: Creates a Bomb Star with a 3×3 blast radius!

🧊 Obstacles

  • Ice Blocks: Match adjacent gems to shatter the ice!
  • Wooden Crates: Reinforced boxes that take 2 hits to destroy.
  • Jelly Tiles: Match gems on top of purple jelly to cleanse it.
