Mechanical Reference

Gear Transmission Types Comparison

Key trade-offs across common industrial gear configurations.

Gear Type Shaft Orientation Single-Stage Ratio Efficiency Noise / Vibration Primary Applications
Spur Gears Parallel 1:1 – 6:1 98% – 99% Moderate (Impact noise) Conveyors, clocks, machine tools
Helical Gears Parallel / Crossed 1:1 – 10:1 96% – 98% Low (Gradual meshing) Automotive transmissions, high-speed drives
Herringbone (Double-Helical) Parallel 1:1 – 12:1 97% – 99% Very Low (Zero thrust) Heavy steel mills, marine turbines
Bevel Gears (Straight / Spiral) Intersecting 90° 1:1 – 6:1 95% – 98% Low to Moderate Differential drives, right-angle reducers
Hypoid Gears Non-intersecting Offset 90° 3:1 – 10:1 90% – 95% Very Low (Quiet) Automotive rear axles, truck differentials
Worm Gears Skew Non-intersecting 90° 5:1 – 70:1 50% – 85% Silent (Self-locking) Elevators, winches, precision steering
Epicyclic Planetary Coaxial In-line 3:1 – 100:1 94% – 97% Low (Load sharing) Wind turbines, robotics, aerospace actuators
